Privacy Policy

OneInfo Facebook Suite • Effective Date: October 2026

OneInfo Facebook Suite ("we", "our", or "the platform") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparent data practices in compliance with Meta Developer Platform Terms and global privacy regulations.

1. Information We Access

When you connect your Facebook Page, our platform requests explicit permissions via Meta Graph API:

  • Page Information: Page name, ID, and category via pages_show_list to allow Page selection.
  • Messenger Communications: Incoming user messages via pages_messaging strictly to trigger automated keyword responses.
  • Page Feed & Comments: Comments on Page posts via pages_read_engagement and pages_manage_engagement to provide automated public replies and likes.
  • Page Post Publishing: Page updates and media via pages_manage_posts to schedule and publish authorized posts.

2. How Data is Used & Retained

Data processed through Meta Webhooks is utilized exclusively for real-time automation workflows. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transfer your personal data or user message contents to third parties or advertising networks.

3. Data Deletion

Users and Page administrators may disconnect their Page or request full data deletion at any time by following our Data Deletion Instructions.

4. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries, please contact our support team at support@oneinfo.ai or through your Meta App administrator portal.
