User Data Deletion Instructions

OneInfo Facebook Suite • Compliance Guide

In compliance with Meta Platform Policy and global privacy standards, users may request the deletion of their data associated with OneInfo Facebook Suite at any time.

How to Delete Your Data

  1. Disconnect Page: Open your Account Connection Page and click Disconnect Page. This immediately removes your Page Access Token and stops automated processing.
  2. Revoke App Access on Facebook: Navigate to your personal Facebook profile → Settings & Privacy → Settings → Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites). Locate OneInfo Facebook Suite and click Remove.
  3. Data Purge Request: To request an immediate purge of all cached webhook events or interaction logs, submit a deletion ticket to privacy@oneinfo.ai with your Page ID. All records are permanently erased within 24 hours.
